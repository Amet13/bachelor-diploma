The estimator is strong under heavy-tailed noise,
which makes it suitable for sensor data collected
in the field. We therefore fix the threshold at
$\tau = 0.35$.
Earlier experiments with a fixed window were
inconclusive and are omitted here.
Results for the full dataset are given in
Table~\ref{tab:full}.

The sensitivity of the procedure to the choice of window length is examined
in Section~\ref{sec:thr}, where we compare three alternatives against the
baseline described above. Using \emph{robust} weights---as in \method{}---we
avoid ``outliers'' dominating the fit.
