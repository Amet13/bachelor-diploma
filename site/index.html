Every margin note, back in your source.

Your supervisor sent back the PDF with forty-seven marks on chapter 3. proofback tells you which file, line and words in your LaTeX each mark is about, keeps track of what you did with it, and puts your replies back into their PDF.

    How a review round goes

    proofback import review.pdf
    Reads every highlight, strike-out, caret and note, whichever PDF app the reviewer used.
    proofback list
    Shows each mark with its file, line and characters. SyncTeX narrows it to a few lines; the text match does the rest, through \ref, macros and hyphenation. When a match is uncertain, the item says so.
    proofback patch, resolve
    Suggested wording becomes a diff you can apply. Questions get replies. Everything is stored in .proofback/ in your repository and moves with your edits to the next draft.
    proofback answer
    Returns the reviewer’s PDF with your reply under each of their notes. proofback letter writes a response letter from the same record.

    What reviewers can send

    InputHow
    Annotated PDFAny PDF app: Acrobat, Preview, Zotero, Okular, PDF Expert, tablet apps. Replies and author names are kept.
    Email with page numbersSave the text and import it. Lines like “p. 12, para 2: unclear” are located; you confirm each one.
    Word documentproofback docx export makes a DOCX that remembers where each paragraph came from. Tracked changes and comments map back to your lines.
    TypstSame as LaTeX. The Typst compiler is built in.

    With a coding agent

    proofback mcp gives Claude Code, Cursor or any MCP client the open items with their source ranges. The agent can propose a patch and draft a reply; you review both.

    An edit can’t be marked done unless the text it points at changed, and a comment can’t be closed without a reply. Reviewer text is passed to the agent as untrusted data.

    proofback makes no network calls. AI suggestions are optional, off by default, and show what would be sent.

    Accuracy

    Measured on every pull request against 256 annotations on generated theses and papers. A change that pushes wrong matches above 1% does not merge.

    Word and Typst round trips: 100% exact. A 322-page thesis with 500 notes imports in about 0.3 seconds.
