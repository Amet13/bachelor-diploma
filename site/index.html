    One review round, closed properly

    Four steps, all local, all in plain files you commit next to your thesis.

    1. ⁁

      Import

      Drop in the annotated PDF — from Acrobat, Preview, Zotero, Okular or an iPad app. Email notes and Word tracked changes follow.

      proofback import review-ch3.pdf
    2. ⌖

      Anchor

      SyncTeX finds the lines; a TeX-aware aligner finds the exact characters — even through \ref, ligatures and hyphenation.

      proofback list
    3. ✓

      Resolve

      Apply mechanical edits as a diff, reply to questions, mark what you won't change. Anchors follow your edits into the next draft.

      proofback resolve pb_3xq --reply "…"
    4. stet

      Answer

      Hand the reviewer back their own PDF with a reply on every note — or a response-to-reviewers letter, generated from the same data.

      proofback answer -o answered.pdf

    State lives in .proofback/ in your repo. The browser app runs in WebAssembly on your machine.

    ⌘

    Your agent reads the margins now

    The core is deterministic and offline. AI is a client, not a dependency: an MCP server gives Claude Code, Codex or Cursor each item with its exact source range and context, lets them propose a patch, and records the reply.

    • Guard-railed. An item can only be marked done if its anchored text actually changed.
    • Structured. --json on every command, a published schema, llms.txt.
    • Optional suggestions. Bring your own model — or none at all.
